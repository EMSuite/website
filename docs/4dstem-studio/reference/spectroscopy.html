Spectroscopy

Applies to 4DSTEM Studio 5.4 and later

Spectroscopy works on an EDS spectrum image, alone or beside the 4D scan of the same area. This page lists every control of its EDS tool.

The workspace needs a spectrum image. Open one with Open Spectrum Image…. The window then shows the element maps, the spectrum of the selected region below them, and the results beside the spectrum. Quantify in the header fits the spectrum of the region.

Not yet validated against reference data

The fit is checked against exSpy, but the compositions have not yet been validated against standards of known composition. Treat at% and wt% as a first estimate, and check them against a standard measured under the same conditions before you report them.

Maps

Control Choices What it does
Maps Net, wt%, at%, Thickness What the tiles show: the net counts of each line window, the composition from the k-factors, or the mass thickness in kg/m²
Filter Off, 3 × 3 average, Gaussian σ 1 px Smooths the wt%, at% and Thickness maps for display. It is on by default, and a filtered tile says so. Net maps are never filtered.

The filter changes the picture only. The readout, the exports and the results use the raw values.

Elements

Pick the elements in the periodic table of the inspector. Auto ID proposes elements from the spectrum of the region. Treat the proposal as a start: remove what cannot be in the sample, and add what you know is there.

Tools

Tool What it does
Select Click a region to select it, drag to move the view. ⌫ deletes the selected region, the arrow keys move it.
Rectangle Drag a rectangle on any map to sum its pixels
Ellipse Drag to draw the ellipse inside a rectangle
Polygon Click the corners on any map; click the first corner to close the polygon
Line Drag a line on any map: each map is plotted along it, in a small chart over the spectrum

With a shape tool, Return draws the shape in the middle of the map.

With a 4D scan of the same area open, Source pools the spectrum over a phase or a diffraction group of the scan instead of Drawn regions.

Quantification

Control Choices What it does
Continuum Empirical, Polynomial The background under the peaks. Empirical fits a continuum over the whole spectrum with the step of the Al K edge. Polynomial is the whole-range polynomial of sixth order, as in exSpy.
k-factors Computed, Typed Computed uses k-factors from Bote-Salvat cross sections, the Krause yield and the EPQ detector efficiency. Typed uses the Cliff-Lorimer k-factors you give, with their source. Edit k-factors… opens them.
Maps method Cliff-Lorimer, Zeta factor, Cross section How the wt% and at% maps are quantified. The zeta factor and the cross section method need the dose and their factors: Edit factors…. The results table is always Cliff-Lorimer.
Beam energy keV Read from the file. Type it when the file does not state it.
Absorption off, on Corrects for absorption in the sample, weighted over the four detectors. It needs the thickness and the detector geometry of the file.
Thickness, Thickness ± nm The sample thickness for the absorption correction, and its uncertainty
Fit quality How well the last fit describes the spectrum

When the absorption correction cannot be applied, the inspector says "Absorption not applied:" and the reason. The composition is then uncorrected.

Fitting

Control Choices What it does
Estimator Least squares, Poisson ML How the model is fitted to the counts
σ_k % The relative uncertainty of a computed k-factor. It is carried into the uncertainties of the composition.
Poly order 0 to 8 With the polynomial continuum: its order
Lock energy axis off, on On: the energy offset, gain and peak width are not refined on the pooled spectrum
Fit to keV The upper end of the fitted range. Empty means the default: the end of the axis or the beam energy, but at most 20 keV.

Results

The table beside the spectrum shows the net counts of the picked elements before Quantify, and the composition in at% and wt% after it, each with its uncertainty. The reference element is marked "ref.": its computed k-factor is 1 by definition, and the others are relative to it. A value marked "not a measurement" has a note that says why. The line under the table counts the notes of the fit and of Auto ID; read them before you use a number.

Export

Button What it writes
Results CSV… The results table: element, line, net counts, at% and wt%, with the record of the fit
Method JSON… The quantification method, with its SHA-256
Spectrum CSV…, Spectrum MSA… The spectrum on screen as energy and counts, or as an EMSA/MAS file
Regions CSV… The pixels, counts, net counts and composition of each kept region
Maps… The maps as one PNG. Scale bar and Regions add the scale bar and the selected region.
Composition by Class… With a 4D scan: every phase of the scan fitted with this method, one row per class, as CSV

The results and the method need Quantify first. Export says what every export carries.

Method

Cliff and Lorimer, Journal of Microscopy 103 (1975) 203, for the composition from intensity ratios. The fit follows exSpy, the reference the numbers are checked against; the functions and their tolerances are listed in SpectroKit's parity notes.
