4DSTEM Studio

4D-STEM analysis on the Mac: from the open scan to calibrated virtual images, Bragg disks, strain and crystal maps.

  • Install: requirements, download, updating and removing the app.
  • First look: a first virtual image within minutes, with the demo dataset.

Your data

  • Export: figures, data files and tables.

Tutorials

The tutorials are being written. They will follow the app's workspaces from top to bottom:

Workspace What the tutorial covers
Prepare Inspecting the dataset and calibrating it: origin, distortion, rotation, pixel size
Imaging Virtual bright-field and dark-field images, diffraction from a scan region
Bragg Disks Detecting the disks in every pattern and judging the detection
Crystal Maps Strain, orientation and phases from the detected disks
Spectroscopy EDS spectrum images, alone or beside the 4D scan
Results Reviewing, saving and exporting

Reference

Every control of a workspace, under the name the app shows.

  • Prepare: the calibration steps, what each unlocks, and how to read the fits.
  • Imaging: virtual imaging, diffraction from a region, and diffraction groups.
  • Bragg Disks: the probe kernel, the detector, and every detection setting with its default.
  • Strain: the reference, the basis, the components and the fit diagnostics.
  • Orientation: the area, the phase model, the quality levels and the display modes.
  • Phase mapping: the phases, the matrix, the matching settings and the evidence.
  • Reconstruction: DPC and iDPC, parallax and ptychography, and what each needs.
  • Spectroscopy: EDS maps, elements, regions, quantification and exports.
  • Results: review, save, compare and export.
