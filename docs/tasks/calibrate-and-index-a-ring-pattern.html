Calibrate and index a ring pattern

Applies to Electron Microscopy Studio 2.1 and later

Take the scale from a ring of a standard, then read which reflections your rings and spots are.

  1. Open the pattern with Open Image… (⌘O) and choose the Diffraction workspace.
  2. With the Ring tool, drag from the inner to the outer edge of a ring. Pick the Standard (Au, Al or Si) and the ring's family, then click Calibrate on Ring. The inspector gives the camera length and how far the fitted scale is from the file's.
  3. Choose a built-in Crystal or Open CIF…, then fit the rings and click the spots of your sample. Each is matched to a reflection within the Tolerance.

Export Readout CSV… writes the rings and spots. Diffraction lists every control.
