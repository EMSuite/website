# Simulate a diffraction pattern from a CIF

Simulate the electron diffraction spots of any crystal from a CIF or a Materials Project phase on a Mac, down any zone axis, with double diffraction.

Applies to Electron Microscopy Studio 2.3 and later.
<p class="lede">See which spots a crystal gives down a zone axis before you tilt to it, or over your own pattern.</p>

1. In the Diffraction workspace, click <span class="ui">Add Phase…</span> and choose a phase from the Materials Project or <span class="ui">Open CIF…</span>. Set the <span class="ui">Voltage</span> and the camera.
2. Type a <span class="ui">Zone axis</span>, such as `110`, and an <span class="ui">In-plane rotation</span>. Drag the crystal or the pattern to tilt it.
3. In <span class="ui">Simulation</span>, switch on <span class="ui">Labels</span> for hkl and <span class="ui">Double diffraction</span> for the spots a crystal forbids but two beams add. Leave the <span class="ui">Excitation error</span> at 0.02 Å⁻¹ unless you know better.

<span class="ui">Spots CSV…</span> writes the table and <span class="ui">Figure…</span> the picture. [Diffraction](/docs/electron-microscopy-studio/reference/diffraction.html) lists every control.
