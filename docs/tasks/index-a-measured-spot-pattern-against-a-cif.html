Index a measured spot pattern against a CIF

Applies to Electron Microscopy Studio 2.3 and later

From a spot pattern to the crystal's zone axis and orientation, with the spots that explain it.

  1. Open the pattern with Open Image… (⌘O) and choose the Diffraction workspace. Check that Calibration shows a scale and the beam energy.
  2. In Phases, click Add Phase…. Search the Materials Project by elements or formula, or click Open CIF….
  3. Click Index (⌘R). The header gives the zone axis, the tilt from it, how many spots it explains and the misfit. A tie or a half turn about the beam is stated, never hidden.

Delete a false spot or click a missed one, then index again. Diffraction lists every control.
