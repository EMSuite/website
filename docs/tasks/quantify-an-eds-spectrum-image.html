Quantify an EDS spectrum image

Applies to Electron Microscopy Studio 2.1 and later

Element maps and a composition from Velox, GMS, Bruker, EDAX and JEOL EDS files.

  1. Open the spectrum image with Open Image… (⌘O). It opens in Spectroscopy › EDS with its maps and spectrum.
  2. Pick the elements in the periodic table of the inspector. Auto ID proposes some; remove what cannot be in the sample. Draw a region on any map.
  3. Click Quantify. The table gives at% and wt% with uncertainties (Cliff-Lorimer), and the maps can show wt% and at%.

A first estimate

The fit is checked against exSpy, but the compositions are not yet validated against standards. Check them against a standard measured under the same conditions before you report them.
