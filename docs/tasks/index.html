What do you want to do?

Applies to Electron Microscopy Studio 2.6 and later

One page per task, each in three steps, with a link to the full reference.

  • Open DM3, DM4 and EMD files on a MacCalibrated TEM images from DigitalMicrograph and Velox, without Windows.
  • Measure d-spacings from an HRTEM imageThe Fourier transform of a lattice image, its spots and their spacings.
  • Calibrate and index a ring patternThe scale from a gold, aluminium or silicon standard, then the rings of your crystal.
  • Quantify an EDS spectrum imageElement maps and a composition in at% and wt% from TEM EDS, and the line intensities of an SEM spectrum.
  • Index EBSD patterns on a MacHough or dictionary indexing of a pattern file, then the orientation map.
  • Index a measured spot pattern against a CIFThe zone axis and orientation of a single-crystal pattern.
  • Simulate a diffraction pattern from a CIFThe spots of any crystal down any zone axis, with double diffraction.
