# Index a measured spot pattern against a CIF

Find the zone axis and orientation of a single-crystal electron diffraction pattern against a CIF or a Materials Project phase on a Mac.

Applies to Electron Microscopy Studio 2.3 and later.
<p class="lede">From a spot pattern to the crystal's zone axis and orientation, with the spots that explain it.</p>

1. Open the pattern with <span class="ui">Open Image…</span> (<kbd>⌘O</kbd>) and choose the Diffraction workspace. Check that <span class="ui">Calibration</span> shows a scale and the beam energy.
2. In <span class="ui">Phases</span>, click <span class="ui">Add Phase…</span>. Search the Materials Project by elements or formula, or click <span class="ui">Open CIF…</span>.
3. Click <span class="ui">Index</span> (<kbd>⌘R</kbd>). The header gives the zone axis, the tilt from it, how many spots it explains and the misfit. A tie or a half turn about the beam is stated, never hidden.

Delete a false spot or click a missed one, then index again. [Diffraction](/docs/electron-microscopy-studio/reference/diffraction.html) lists every control.
