Simulate a diffraction pattern from a CIF

Applies to Electron Microscopy Studio 2.6 and later

See which spots a crystal gives down a zone axis before you tilt to it, or over your own pattern.

  1. Choose File › New Simulation. In Phases, click Add Phase… and pick a Materials Project phase or open a CIF. Set the Voltage and the Camera length in Microscope.
  2. Type a Zone axis, such as 110, and an In-plane rotation, or drag the crystal or the pattern to tilt it.
  3. In Simulation, switch on hkl labels, and Double diffraction for the spots a crystal forbids but two beams add. The Excitation error starts at 0.02 Å⁻¹, as py4DSTEM. Intensities picks kinematic or dynamical spots.

Spots CSV… writes the table and Figure… the picture. Diffraction lists every control.
