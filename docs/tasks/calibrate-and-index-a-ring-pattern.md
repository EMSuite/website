# Calibrate and index a ring pattern

Calibrate an electron diffraction pattern on a gold, aluminium or silicon ring, then index the rings and spots against a crystal or a CIF.

Applies to Electron Microscopy Studio 2.1 and later.
<p class="lede">Take the scale from a ring of a standard, then read which reflections your rings and spots are.</p>

1. Open the pattern with <span class="ui">Open Image…</span> (<kbd>⌘O</kbd>) and choose the Diffraction workspace.
2. With the <span class="ui">Ring</span> tool, drag from the inner to the outer edge of a ring. Pick the <span class="ui">Standard</span> (Au, Al or Si) and the ring's family, then click <span class="ui">Calibrate on Ring</span>. The inspector gives the camera length and how far the fitted scale is from the file's.
3. Choose a built-in <span class="ui">Crystal</span> or <span class="ui">Open CIF…</span>, then fit the rings and click the spots of your sample. Each is matched to a reflection within the <span class="ui">Tolerance</span>.

<span class="ui">Export Readout CSV…</span> writes the rings and spots. [Diffraction](/docs/electron-microscopy-studio/reference/diffraction.html) lists every control.
