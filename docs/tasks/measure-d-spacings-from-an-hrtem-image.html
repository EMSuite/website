Measure d-spacings from an HRTEM image

Applies to Electron Microscopy Studio 2.1 and later

The Fourier transform of a lattice image has a pair of spots for each set of fringes. Electron Microscopy Studio measures each spot and gives its spacing.

  1. Open the image with Open Image… (⌘O) and choose FFT under Imaging in the sidebar.
  2. Drag a square region over the crystal. A window tapers its edges, so the edge of the region does not streak the transform.
  3. Click Find Spots, or click a spot yourself. Each spot is listed with its d-spacing and its |q|; ⚠ marks a spot with another peak close by.

The spacing is in nm when the image is calibrated, and in pixels when it is not. A masked inverse shows where in the image the frequencies of chosen spots come from. FFT lists every control.
