Electron Microscopy Studio

TEM and SEM data on the Mac: images, diffraction, EBSD and spectra, each in its own workspace.

Get started

  • Install: requirements, download, updating and removing the app.
  • First look: a first measurement within minutes, with the demo data.
  • The window: where everything is, and the keyboard shortcuts.

Your data

  • File formats: what opens, and the files that are read together.
  • Sessions: how your measurements are saved beside the image.
  • Export: pictures, tables and the method, part by part.

Reference

Every control of a part, under the name the app shows.

  • Image: calibration, display, measuring, particles and stacks.
  • FFT: the region, spots, the radial profile, the masked inverse and the HRTEM filters.
  • Diffraction: the centre, the calibration on a standard, rings, spots and the crystal.
  • EBSD Patterns: virtual images and the processing stages.
  • EBSD Indexing: phases, Hough and dictionary indexing, and refinement.
  • EBSD Maps: the map layers, phases, grains and texture.
  • EDS: maps, elements, quantification and SEM spectra.
  • EELS: thickness, deconvolution, edges and fitting.
  • Results: the table of every measurement and its export.

Tutorials

The tutorials are being written. They will follow the app's workspaces and their parts from top to bottom:

Workspace Part What the tutorial covers
Imaging Image The calibrated image: contrast, distances, angles and profiles
Imaging FFT The Fourier transform, a live FFT on a region, lattice spacings
Diffraction Camera length, d-spacings, indexing and zone axis
EBSD Patterns EBSD and TKD patterns: the scan and its pattern processing
EBSD Indexing Hough and dictionary indexing of the scan
EBSD Maps Orientation and phase maps, grains and pole figures
Spectroscopy EDS Spectrum images: elements, maps and composition
Spectroscopy EELS Thickness maps, core-loss edges and composition
Results Reviewing and exporting the numbers
