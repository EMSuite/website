Tutorials

The tutorials are being written. They will follow the app's workspaces and their parts from top to bottom:

Workspace Part What the tutorial covers
Imaging Image The calibrated image: contrast, distances, angles and profiles
Imaging FFT The Fourier transform, a live FFT on a region, lattice spacings
Diffraction Camera length, d-spacings, indexing and zone axis
EBSD Patterns EBSD and TKD patterns: the scan and its pattern processing
EBSD Indexing Hough and dictionary indexing of the scan
EBSD Maps Orientation and phase maps, grains and pole figures
Spectroscopy EDS Spectrum images: elements, maps and composition
Spectroscopy EELS Thickness maps, core-loss edges and composition
Results Reviewing and exporting the numbers
