Electron Microscopy Studio · Reference

FFT

Applies to Electron Microscopy Studio 2.6 and later

FFT shows the Fourier transform of an image or of a region of it, measures its spots, and filters the image through a mask. This page lists every control of the part, which is in the Imaging workspace.

Region

Control What it does
Transformed What is transformed: the whole image, or the region with its position and size
Use Centred Square Sets the largest centred square as the region
Clear Region Transforms the whole image again
Δq, or Δq (x) and Δq (y) The frequency step between neighbouring bins of the transform

Draw a region on the image to transform only that part.

Before the transform

Control Choices What it does
Window Tapers the edges of the region before the transform. A hard edge streaks the spectrum along both axes.
Smoothing None, Gaussian with σ Smooths the region first
Band-pass None, Difference of Gaussians, Butterworth high-pass, Butterworth low-pass Keeps a band of frequencies. Low σ or Cutoff sets it.

The inspector sums the steps up in one line that starts "Before the transform:". The picture is drawn as log₁₀ of the power. The window, the smoothing and the band-pass change the transform the spots are measured on: they change a spot's power, not its position.

Spots

Click a spot on the transform to measure it. The spot is listed in the inspector with its values.

Control What it does
Find Spots Puts the strongest reflections on the transform, each measured as a click would be
Threshold, Distance, Most spots The finder's settings: the level as a fraction of the range, the least distance between spots in bins, and the most spots it places
Measure at… Measures at a position you type, kx and ky in bins
In Mask Passes this spot in the masked inverse. Only a measured spot can be in the mask.
Remove, Remove All Deletes one spot, or all

A yellow ring is a measured spot, a blue one the selected spot, and a dashed one a spot that could not be measured. ⚠ marks a spot with another peak close by: its position is less certain.

Strain (GPA)

Appears once a spot is measured. Strain and rotation maps by geometric phase analysis, from the first two measured spots at an angle taken as g₁ and g₂. Reference chooses the part of the region taken as undistorted, whose mean strain and rotation are zero; Map Strain computes the maps and shows them in the left pane. The section states its validation: the maps are checked on planted fields only. The transform also exports as a PDF or SVG figure with its rings and labels as vectors (Export).

Radial Profile

The mean power against |q|. Point at the chart to read a bin.

Masked Inverse

Appears once a spot is measured. The inverse transform through a mask around the spots marked In Mask shows where in the image those frequencies come from.

Control Unit What it does
Radius bins The mask passes everything within this distance of the centre of a spot
Edge bins The mask falls to nothing over this distance beyond the radius

HRTEM Filters

A re-implementation of D. R. G. Mitchell's HRTEM filter script for Digital Micrograph (ABSF and Wiener). Parameters holds the settings, Filter runs it, and Last run says what the run did.

Filtered images are for looking, not for measuring

The app states the limits itself. A lattice that falls between two Fourier bins loses amplitude: on synthetic lattices it keeps 5 to 57 % in a 128 px region and 77 to 97 % at 256 px. Intensities are not preserved: the mean of the output is below that of the input.

Display

View chooses what the image pane shows, for example the image itself or the masked inverse.
