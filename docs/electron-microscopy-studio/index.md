# Electron Microscopy Studio

Documentation for Electron Microscopy Studio, the native macOS application for TEM and SEM data: getting started, data, tutorials, reference and troubleshooting.
<p class="lede">TEM and SEM data on the Mac: images, diffraction, EBSD and spectra, each in its own workspace.</p>

- [First look](/docs/electron-microscopy-studio/first-look.html): a first measurement within minutes, with the demo data.
- [The window](/docs/electron-microscopy-studio/window.html): where everything is, and the keyboard shortcuts.

## Your data

- [File formats](/docs/electron-microscopy-studio/data/formats.html): what opens, and the files that are read together.

## Reference

Every control of a part, under the name the app shows.

- [Image](/docs/electron-microscopy-studio/reference/image.html): calibration, display, measuring, particles and stacks.
- [FFT](/docs/electron-microscopy-studio/reference/fft.html): the region, spots, the radial profile, the masked inverse and the HRTEM filters.
- [Diffraction](/docs/electron-microscopy-studio/reference/diffraction.html): the centre, the calibration on a standard, rings, spots and the crystal.
- [EBSD Patterns](/docs/electron-microscopy-studio/reference/ebsd-patterns.html): virtual images and the processing stages.
- [EBSD Indexing](/docs/electron-microscopy-studio/reference/ebsd-indexing.html): phases, Hough and dictionary indexing, and refinement.
- [EBSD Maps](/docs/electron-microscopy-studio/reference/ebsd-maps.html): the map layers, phases, grains and texture.
- [EDS](/docs/electron-microscopy-studio/reference/eds.html): maps, elements, quantification and SEM spectra.
- [EELS](/docs/electron-microscopy-studio/reference/eels.html): thickness, deconvolution, edges and fitting.
- [Results](/docs/electron-microscopy-studio/reference/results.html): the table of every measurement and its export.

## Tutorials

The tutorials are being written. They will follow the app's workspaces and their parts from top to bottom:

| Workspace | Part | What the tutorial covers |
|---|---|---|
| Imaging | Image | The calibrated image: contrast, distances, angles and profiles |
| Imaging | FFT | The Fourier transform, a live FFT on a region, lattice spacings |
| Diffraction | | Camera length, d-spacings, indexing and zone axis |
| EBSD | Patterns | EBSD and TKD patterns: the scan and its pattern processing |
| EBSD | Indexing | Hough and dictionary indexing of the scan |
| EBSD | Maps | Orientation and phase maps, grains and pole figures |
| Spectroscopy | EDS | Spectrum images: elements, maps and composition |
| Spectroscopy | EELS | Thickness maps, core-loss edges and composition |
| Results | | Reviewing and exporting the numbers |
